Unit 2: Productivity and Work Study

Introduction

Productivity is a key measure of efficiency in any organization. It indicates how effectively resources such as labor, materials, machines, and capital are utilized to produce goods and services. Higher productivity leads to lower costs, higher profits, improved competitiveness, and better customer satisfaction.

Work Study is a scientific technique used to improve productivity by analyzing how work is performed and finding better methods to complete tasks efficiently. It consists of Method Study and Work Measurement, which help organizations eliminate waste, improve processes, and establish performance standards.

Topic 1: Productivity

Explanation

Productivity refers to the relationship between output produced and inputs used.

It measures how efficiently resources are utilized to generate products or services.

Productivity Formula

Productivity = Output / Input

A higher productivity ratio indicates better utilization of resources.

TypeDescription
Labour ProductivityOutput per worker
Machine ProductivityOutput per machine
Material ProductivityOutput per unit of material
Capital ProductivityOutput per unit of capital
Total ProductivityOutput compared to total inputs

Factors Affecting Productivity

  • Employee skills
  • Technology used
  • Quality of materials
  • Management efficiency
  • Working conditions
  • Training and motivation

Example 1 (Business): A factory produces 1,000 units using 100 labor hours. If it later produces 1,200 units with the same labor hours, productivity has increased.

Example 2 (Daily Life): A student who studies 3 chapters in 2 hours instead of 2 chapters in 2 hours has improved productivity.

Formula for Standard Time

Standard Time = Normal Time × (1 + Allowance Rate)

Numerical Example

Observed Time = 10 minutes
Performance Rating = 120%
Allowance = 15%

Normal Time
= 10 × 120/100
= 12 minutes

Standard Time
= 12 × 1.15
= 13.8 minutes

Topic 12: Synthetic Time Standards

Synthetic Time Standards are predetermined standards developed from previously measured work elements.

  • Saves time
  • Reduces study costs
  • Provides consistency
  • Useful for repetitive jobs

Quick Revision Summary

  • Productivity measures efficiency of resource utilization.
  • Work Study consists of Method Study and Work Measurement.
  • Method Study improves work procedures.
  • Flow Process Charts help analyze processes.
  • Flow Diagrams track physical movement.
  • Process Mapping visualizes workflows.
  • Performance Rating converts observed time into normal time.
  • Allowances account for fatigue and delays.
  • Standard Time includes normal time and allowances.
  • Work Sampling uses statistical observation techniques.
