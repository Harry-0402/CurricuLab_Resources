Unit 1: Law of Contract – Indian Contract Act, 1872

Introduction

The Indian Contract Act, 1872 is one of the most important business laws in India. It governs agreements and contracts entered into by individuals and organizations. Every business transaction, whether buying goods, hiring employees, obtaining loans, or entering into partnerships, is based on contractual relationships.

The Act defines the essentials of a valid contract, rights and obligations of parties, remedies for breach of contract, and various special contracts such as indemnity, guarantee, bailment, pledge, and agency.

A sound understanding of contract law helps businesses avoid disputes and ensures legal protection in commercial dealings.

Topic 1: Nature of Contracts

Explanation

A contract is a legally enforceable agreement between two or more parties.

According to Section 2(h) of the Indian Contract Act, 1872:

"An agreement enforceable by law is a contract."

Nature of Contract

  • Creates legal obligations.
  • Involves two or more parties.
  • Based on mutual consent.
  • Enforceable by courts.
  • Intended to create legal relationships.

Topic 4: Offer and Acceptance

Explanation

Offer

An offer is a proposal made by one person to another expressing willingness to do or abstain from doing something.

Acceptance

Acceptance means giving consent to the offer.

Rules of Valid Offer

  • Must be clear and definite.
  • Must be communicated.
  • Must create legal obligations.

Rules of Acceptance

  • Must be absolute and unconditional.
  • Must be communicated.
  • Must be made within prescribed time.

Topic 5: Consideration

Explanation

Consideration refers to something of value exchanged between parties.

"No consideration, no contract."

  • Must be lawful.
  • May be past, present, or future.
  • Must move at the desire of the promisor.

Topic 6: Capacity to Contract

Explanation

Only competent persons can enter into valid contracts.

Persons Competent to Contract

  • Major (18 years or above)
  • Sound mind
  • Not disqualified by law

Persons Not Competent

  • Minors
  • Persons of unsound mind
  • Disqualified persons

Topic 7: Free Consent

Explanation

Consent is free when it is not caused by:

  • Coercion
  • Undue Influence
  • Fraud
  • Misrepresentation
  • Mistake

Topic 8: Legality of Object

Explanation

The object and consideration of a contract must be lawful.

  • Illegal activities
  • Fraudulent purposes
  • Activities against public policy
  • Activities prohibited by law

Topic 9: Types of Contracts

Explanation

Express Contract

Terms clearly stated orally or in writing.

Implied Contract

Created through conduct.

Quasi Contract

Created by law to prevent unjust enrichment.

Topic 10: Contingent Contracts

Explanation

A contingent contract depends on the occurrence or non-occurrence of a future uncertain event.

  • Based on uncertainty.
  • Performance depends on an event.

Topic 11: Performance of Contract

Explanation

Performance means fulfilling contractual obligations.

Modes of Performance

  • Actual Performance
  • Attempted Performance (Tender)

Topic 12: Discharge of Contract

Explanation

Discharge means termination of contractual obligations.

ModeMeaning
PerformanceCompletion of obligations
Mutual AgreementParties agree to terminate
ImpossibilityPerformance becomes impossible
BreachContract violated
Operation of LawLegal termination

Topic 13: Quasi Contracts

Explanation

Quasi Contracts are obligations imposed by law even though no actual contract exists.

Purpose

To prevent unjust enrichment.

Example Situations

  • Supply of necessities
  • Payment by interested person
  • Finder of lost goods

Topic 14: Remedies for Breach of Contract

Explanation

When a contract is breached, the injured party can seek legal remedies.

  1. Damages
  2. Specific Performance
  3. Injunction
  4. Quantum Meruit
  5. Rescission

Topic 15: Contract of Indemnity

Explanation

An indemnity contract protects one party from loss caused by another.

  • Indemnifier
  • Indemnified

Topic 16: Contract of Guarantee

Explanation

A guarantee is a promise to perform the obligation of a third person if that person defaults.

PartyRole

Topic 17: Bailment and Pledge

A. Bailment

Transfer of possession of goods for a specific purpose with an agreement to return them.

  • Bailor
  • Bailee

B. Pledge

A pledge is bailment of goods as security for repayment of debt.

  • Pawnor
  • Pawnee

Topic 18: Law of Agency

Explanation

Agency arises when one person acts on behalf of another.

PartyMeaning
PrincipalPerson represented
AgentPerson acting on behalf of principal

Creation of Agency

  • Express Agreement
  • Implied Agreement
  • Necessity
  • Ratification

Quick Revision Summary

  • A contract is an agreement enforceable by law.
  • Essential elements include offer, acceptance, consideration, capacity, and free consent.
  • Consideration is the price paid for a promise.
  • Minors are not competent to contract.
  • Consent must be free from coercion, fraud, and undue influence.
  • Contracts may be express, implied, contingent, or quasi.
  • Contracts may be discharged by performance, breach, or impossibility.
  • Remedies include damages and specific performance.
  • Indemnity protects against loss.
  • Guarantee involves surety, creditor, and principal debtor.
  • Bailment transfers possession, while pledge provides security for debt.
  • Agency allows one person to act on behalf of another.
