Unit 4: Laws of Insurance, Insolvency, Carriage of Goods and Arbitration

Unit Overview

Business activities involve various risks and disputes. Laws relating to Insurance, Insolvency, Carriage of Goods, and Arbitration provide legal protection and ensure smooth commercial transactions. Insurance protects against financial losses, insolvency laws deal with inability to pay debts, carriage laws regulate transportation of goods, and arbitration provides an alternative dispute resolution mechanism.

2. Principles of Insurance

A. Utmost Good Faith (Uberrimae Fidei)

Both parties must disclose all material facts honestly.

Example

A person must disclose existing health problems before taking health insurance.

B. Insurable Interest

The insured must have a financial interest in the subject matter insured.

Example

A person can insure his own house but not a stranger's house.

C. Indemnity

Insurance compensates actual loss and does not allow profit.

Example

A damaged car worth ₹2 lakh cannot receive compensation of ₹5 lakh.

D. Subrogation

After compensation, the insurer obtains rights against third parties responsible for loss.

Example

Insurance company can recover compensation from a negligent transporter.

E. Contribution

When multiple insurers cover the same risk, compensation is shared proportionately.

F. Proximate Cause

Compensation depends on the nearest effective cause of loss.

3. Life Insurance

Life Insurance provides financial protection against risks related to human life.

Features

  • Payment upon death or maturity.
  • Savings and investment element.

Benefits

  • Tax benefits.
  • Wealth creation.

Example

Term insurance policies.

5. Fire Insurance

Definition

Insurance against losses caused by fire and related risks.

Essentials

Insurance covering damage to a factory caused by fire.

Part B: Insolvency Law

7. Insolvency

Definition

Insolvency is the condition where a person cannot pay debts when they become due.

Objectives of Insolvency Law

  • Protect creditors.
  • Ensure fair distribution of assets.
  • Provide relief to honest debtors.
  • Facilitate economic stability.

8. Procedure of Insolvency

9. Property and Debt of Insolvent

Property of Insolvent

All assets of the insolvent person become available for settlement of debts.

Debts

Legal obligations owed to creditors.

Priority

Certain debts receive priority during distribution.

10. Discharge of Insolvent

Definition

Discharge releases the insolvent person from legal liability for unpaid debts.

Effects

  • Fresh financial start.
  • Protection from creditors.
  • Removal of legal restrictions.

Example

Court grants discharge after completion of insolvency proceedings.

Part D: Arbitration

15. Arbitration

Definition

Arbitration is a method of resolving disputes outside courts through an impartial third party called an arbitrator.

Purpose

  • Faster dispute resolution.
  • Lower costs.
  • Confidentiality.
  • Reduced court burden.

16. General Provisions Regarding Arbitration

Parties Agree

Dispute Arises
↓
Appointment of Arbitrator
↓
Hearing
↓

↓

Features

  • Voluntary process.
  • Neutral arbitrator.
  • Binding decision.
  • Less formal than courts.

17. Modes of Arbitration

A. Voluntary Arbitration

Parties mutually agree to arbitration.

Example

Business partners referring disputes to an arbitrator.

B. Institutional Arbitration

Conducted through recognized arbitration institutions.

Example

Arbitration centers and chambers.

C. Ad Hoc Arbitration

Parties independently decide procedures and arbitrators.

Example

Two companies appointing a mutually agreed arbitrator.

Arbitration vs Court Litigation

Important Definitions

  • Insurance – Contract providing financial protection against risk.
  • Insurer – Company providing insurance coverage.
  • Insured – Person receiving protection.
  • Insurable Interest – Financial interest in insured subject matter.
  • Indemnity – Compensation for actual loss.
  • Insolvency – Inability to pay debts.
  • Receiver – Person managing insolvent's assets.
  • Carriage of Goods – Transportation of goods.
  • Bill of Lading – Document used in sea transport.
  • Arbitration – Alternative dispute resolution method.
  • Arbitrator – Neutral person deciding disputes.
