Unit 5: Digital Technologies and Trends

Introduction

Digital technologies are transforming businesses, industries, and societies at an unprecedented pace. Organizations today rely on technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, Cloud Computing, Big Data, and Machine Learning to improve efficiency, enhance customer experiences, and create innovative business models.

Understanding these technologies and their applications is essential for organizations seeking to remain competitive in the digital economy. Successful integration of emerging technologies enables businesses to improve decision-making, automate processes, reduce costs, and drive innovation.

Topic 1: Emerging Technologies

Explanation

Emerging Technologies are new and evolving technologies that have the potential to significantly impact businesses, industries, and society.

Topic 4: Blockchain Technology

Explanation

Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger that records transactions securely across multiple computers.

Once data is recorded, it becomes difficult to alter, ensuring transparency and security.

Topic 5: Cloud Computing

Explanation

Cloud Computing refers to the delivery of computing services such as storage, servers, databases, and software over the internet.

Organizations can access resources without owning physical infrastructure.

Types of Cloud Services

Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)

Provides virtualized computing resources.

Platform as a Service (PaaS)

Provides development platforms.

Software as a Service (SaaS)

Provides software through the internet.

Benefits

  • Cost reduction
  • Scalability
  • Flexibility
  • Remote access
  • Faster deployment
