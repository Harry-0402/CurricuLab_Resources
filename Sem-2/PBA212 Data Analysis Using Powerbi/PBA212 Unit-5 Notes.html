Unit 5: Visualizations and Dashboards

Introduction

Data analysis becomes meaningful only when insights are presented in a clear and understandable manner. Visualizations help convert raw numbers into charts, graphs, and interactive reports that are easier to interpret. Power BI provides a wide variety of visualization tools that enable users to discover trends, compare performance, identify patterns, and make better decisions.

A Dashboard is a collection of visualizations displayed on a single screen that provides a summary of important business information.

Visualization Process

Raw Data ↓ Data Modeling ↓ DAX Calculations ↓ Visualizations ↓ Reports ↓ Dashboards ↓ Business Decisions

Topic 1: Visualizing Data

Explanation

Data Visualization is the graphical representation of information using charts, graphs, maps, tables, and dashboards.

Visualization helps users understand complex data quickly without analyzing large tables of numbers.

Benefits

  • Simplifies complex information.
  • Improves decision-making.
  • Identifies trends and patterns.
  • Enhances communication.
  • Makes reports interactive.

Example 1

A retail company uses charts to analyze monthly sales performance.

Example 2

A student uses graphs to compare marks across subjects.

Topic 2: Why Visualizations?

Explanation

Humans understand visual information much faster than numerical data.

Reasons for Using Visualizations
