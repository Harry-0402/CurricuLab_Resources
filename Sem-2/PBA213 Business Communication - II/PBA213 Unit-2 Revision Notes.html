Unit 2: Group Communication

Unit Overview

Group Communication refers to the exchange of information among three or more people working toward a common objective. In business organizations, group communication takes place through interviews, group discussions, meetings, conferences, and public relations activities. Effective group communication improves teamwork, decision-making, and organizational performance.

Key Concepts

Group Communication

Definition

Group Communication is the process of sharing information, ideas, and opinions among members of a group to achieve common goals.

Characteristics

  • Involves multiple participants.
  • Encourages idea sharing.
  • Supports decision-making.
  • Promotes teamwork.

Example

A project team discussing strategies for launching a new product.

Group Discussion (GD)

Definition

A Group Discussion is a structured conversation among participants on a specific topic to evaluate communication, leadership, and analytical skills.

Objectives

  • Exchange ideas.
  • Evaluate problem-solving ability.
  • Assess communication skills.
  • Develop teamwork.

Types of Group Discussion

Tips for Effective GD

  • Speak clearly and confidently.
  • Listen actively.
  • Respect others' opinions.
  • Avoid interrupting.
  • Support arguments with facts.

Interviews

Definition

An Interview is a formal interaction between an interviewer and an interviewee to gather information or assess suitability.

Preparing for an Interview

Before Interview

  • Research the company.
  • Understand job requirements.
  • Prepare common questions.
  • Dress professionally.
  • Carry necessary documents.

During Interview

  • Maintain eye contact.
  • Listen carefully.
  • Answer confidently.
  • Use positive body language.

After Interview

  • Thank the interviewer.
  • Follow up professionally.

Types of Interviews

1. Selection Interview

Definition

Conducted to select suitable candidates for employment.

Purpose

  • Assess skills.
  • Evaluate knowledge.
  • Determine job suitability.

2. Appraisal Interview

Definition

Conducted to evaluate employee performance.

Objectives

  • Review achievements.
  • Discuss improvement areas.
  • Set future goals.

3. Grievance Interview

Definition

Conducted to address employee complaints and concerns.

Benefits

  • Resolves conflicts.
  • Improves employee satisfaction.
  • Enhances workplace harmony.

4. Exit Interview

Definition

Conducted when an employee leaves an organization.

Purpose

  • Understand reasons for leaving.
  • Gather organizational feedback.
  • Improve retention strategies.

Meetings

A Meeting is a formal gathering of people to discuss issues and make decisions.

Need and Importance of Meetings

Importance

  • Problem-solving.
  • Decision-making.
  • Coordination of activities.
  • Performance review.

Example

Monthly departmental review meeting.

Modern Methods of Conferencing

Video Conferencing

Definition

Communication through video and audio technology over the internet.

Advantages

  • Saves travel costs.
  • Supports remote collaboration.
  • Enables real-time communication.

Examples

  • Zoom
  • Microsoft Teams
  • Google Meet

Teleconferencing

Definition

Communication among participants through telephone networks.

Advantages

  • Fast communication.
  • Cost-effective.
  • Useful for geographically dispersed teams.

Public Relations (PR)

Definition

Public Relations is the strategic communication process that builds and maintains a positive relationship between an organization and its stakeholders.

Stakeholders

  • Customers
  • Employees
  • Investors
  • Government
  • Media
  • Society

Functions of Public Relations Department

Major Functions

  1. Media Relations
  2. Corporate Communication
  3. Crisis Management
  4. Event Management
  5. Brand Image Building
  6. Community Relations

Internal and External Measures of PR

Internal PR

Definition

Communication activities directed toward employees.

Examples

  • Newsletters
  • Employee meetings
  • Training programs

External PR

Definition

Communication activities directed toward external stakeholders.

Examples

  • Press releases
  • Advertisements
  • Social media communication

Internal vs External PR

Feature Internal PR External PR

Importance of Group Communication

Benefits

  • Enhances teamwork.
  • Improves decision-making.
  • Encourages participation.
  • Reduces misunderstandings.
  • Increases organizational effectiveness.

Important Definitions

  • Group Communication – Communication among multiple individuals.
  • Group Discussion – Structured discussion on a specific topic.
  • Interview – Formal interaction for assessment or information gathering.
  • Meeting – Organized gathering for discussion and decisions.
  • Conference – Large-scale formal discussion event.
  • Video Conferencing – Online audio-video communication.
  • Teleconferencing – Communication through telephone networks.
  • Public Relations (PR) – Managing organizational image and relationships.

Exam-Oriented Short Notes

A structured discussion used to evaluate communication, leadership, and analytical abilities.

They facilitate information sharing, coordination, and decision-making.

What is Public Relations?

A communication function that builds and maintains positive stakeholder relationships.

Unit 2 Summary

Group Communication plays a vital role in organizational success. Interviews, meetings, conferences, and public relations activities help organizations communicate effectively, make decisions, solve problems, and maintain strong stakeholder relationships. Effective group communication enhances teamwork, productivity, and professional growth.
