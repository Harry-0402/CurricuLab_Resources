Unit 2: Group Communication

Introduction

Communication in organizations rarely occurs only between two individuals. Most business activities involve interaction among teams, departments, clients, customers, and stakeholders. Group Communication refers to the exchange of information among three or more individuals working toward a common objective.

Effective group communication improves coordination, decision-making, problem-solving, teamwork, and organizational performance.

In modern organizations, group communication takes various forms such as interviews, meetings, conferences, and public relations activities.

Topic 1: Interviews

Explanation

An interview is a formal conversation between two or more people conducted to gather information, assess candidates, evaluate performance, or resolve issues.

Interviews help organizations make informed decisions regarding recruitment, performance management, and employee relations.

Objectives of Interviews

  • Assess knowledge and skills.
  • Evaluate personality and attitude.
  • Gather information.
  • Make employment decisions.
  • Resolve workplace concerns.

Interview Process

Preparation ↓ Questioning ↓ Discussion ↓ Evaluation ↓ Decision Making

Example 1

A company conducts interviews to recruit a Marketing Manager.

Example 2

A college interviews candidates for admission into an MBA program.

Topic 2: Group Discussion (GD)

Explanation

A Group Discussion is a structured conversation among a group of participants on a given topic.

Organizations often use GDs during recruitment to assess communication skills, leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities.

Objectives

  • Evaluate communication skills.
  • Assess leadership qualities.
  • Measure teamwork.
  • Analyze decision-making ability.

Characteristics of a Good GD Participant

  • Active participation
  • Logical thinking
  • Respect for others
  • Confidence
  • Listening skills

Example 1

MBA students discuss the impact of Artificial Intelligence on business.

Example 2

Job applicants discuss the future of e-commerce during campus placement.

Topic 3: Preparing for an Interview

Explanation

Proper preparation significantly increases the chances of interview success.

Steps for Interview Preparation

Research Organization ↓ Understand Job Role ↓ Prepare Answers ↓ Practice Communication ↓ Dress Professionally ↓ Attend Interview

Interview Preparation Tips

  • Study the company profile.
  • Review your resume.
  • Practice common questions.
  • Maintain positive body language.
  • Arrive on time.

Example 1

A candidate studies company products before attending an interview.

Example 2

A student practices mock interviews with friends.

Topic 13: Video Conferencing and Teleconferencing

  • Global collaboration.

Example 2

PR helps build and maintain a positive public image.

Objectives

  • Improve reputation.
  • Manage communication.
  • Strengthen stakeholder relationships.

Example 1

A company issues press releases regarding new product launches.

Example 2

Explanation

PR activities are directed toward both internal and external audiences.

Internal PR

Examples:

External PR

Communication with customers, media, investors, and society.

Examples:

  • Press releases
  • Social media communication
  • Corporate events

Comparison

Internal PR External PR
Employees Customers & Public

Motivation & Awareness Reputation Building

Example 1

Example 2

A media campaign improves brand image.
